The glucose mill carries most of the spend and misses about one delivery in ten. The packaging converter is the cheapest bag supplier but the least reliable (about four in five on time, some short shipments). The chemicals distributor costs more and is never late. That trade-off is why it is the preferred partner rather than the cheaper broadline distributor.

Are we paying more or less than planned?

Purchase price variance = (price paid − standard cost) × quantity received. Standard costs are set each January from December's market prices, so variance shows how markets moved during the year. Above zero = paid more than planned.

In 2024 corn fell after the standard was set, so glucose came in under plan. In 2026 nitrogen chemicals rose all year with the fertilizer market (US producer price index), and variance turned unfavorable.

What did the August 2025 glucose outage cost?

The preferred mill could not deliver for four weeks. Production never stopped: the buyer placed emergency orders with the backup supplier, who delivered in a week at a premium.
