Is supply keeping up with demand?

Customer orders by month against the statistical forecast the plant planned on. About one order in 25 ships a week late: rush orders placed inside the lead time, and shipments held for a carrier or quality release. Forecast bias: -1.4% (positive = demand above forecast).

Is inventory where it should be?

Finished-goods powder on hand at each week's end, against the safety stock set in the ERP's reorder rule. The weekly saw-tooth is batch production: whole batches of about 13.6 tonnes of powder arrive, then ship out over the week. Forward weeks of supply (on hand ÷ the next four weeks' forecast) averaged 1.9 weeks over the last 52 weeks.
