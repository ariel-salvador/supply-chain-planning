# US EPA AP-42 §9.9.7 Corn Wet Milling

- **Citation:** US EPA, *AP-42: Compilation of Air Emissions Factors*, Chapter 9.9.7
  Corn Wet Milling.
- **URL:** https://www.epa.gov/sites/default/files/2020-10/documents/c9s09-7.pdf
- **Retrieved:** 2026-09-18
- **Reliability:** US government reference document. Its mass yields are industry
  averages (older, 1990s context).
- **reviewed:** false

## Key facts (paraphrased)

- An average bushel is 25 kg (56 lb). It yields about 14 kg (32 lb) starch, about 6.6 kg
  (14.5 lb) feed products and about 0.9 kg (2 lb) oil. The rest is water. (§9.9.7.1)
- Roughly 80 % of starch slurry goes to syrups, sugars and ethanol. (§9.9.7.1)
- Dextrose crystallisation recovers about 60 % of the solids per pass (§9.9.7.1). We
  don't use this yet. It's relevant if we move from a corn-only price to a refined
  dextrose price.
