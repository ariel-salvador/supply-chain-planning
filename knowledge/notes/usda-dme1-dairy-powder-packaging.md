# USDA Commodity Requirements DME1: dairy powder packaging (25 kg multiwall bags)

- **Citation:** USDA Commodity Requirements DME1, *Nonfortified Nonfat Dry Milk for Use
  in Export Programs*, effective 2006-01-23.
- **URL:** https://www.fsa.usda.gov/Internet/FSA_File/dme1.pdf (US government work)
- **Retrieved:** 2026-09-18
- **Reliability:** regulatory purchase specification. It covers an **analog** product
  (nonfat dry milk), not rBLG powder. The USDA *whey protein concentrate* grade
  specification (https://www.ams.usda.gov/sites/default/files/media/drywheyconcentrate.pdf)
  was checked and gives no container size.
- **reviewed:** false

## Key facts (paraphrased)
- Product is packed **25 kg per multiwall paper bag** (§2.4 A).
- Bags have at least three kraft-paper walls plus a polyethylene inner liner (§2.4 B).
  They're closed with hot-melt adhesive (§2.5), and there's an alternative to the 3-ply
  bag (§2.6).

## Used in
`bag_net_kg` → packaging line in `products/blg_whey_protein.yaml` (1/25 bag per kg powder).
